THE ARTISTS

Mantana Art

Contemporary Artist

Anthony Mendy, known as Mantana Art, discovered a passion for art at a young age, drawn to the freedom of expressing ideas and emotions through creativity. What began as a simple childhood interest gradually became a deep commitment to the craft.

Over the past decade, Mantana has continued to develop his artistic practice, turning that early passion into a professional creative journey. His work reflects years of learning, experimentation and dedication, with each piece carrying a part of his story and perspective.

For Mantana, art is more than a profession — it is a way of seeing, feeling and connecting with others. His journey is a reminder that a passion nurtured with patience and dedication can grow into something meaningful.

Through a distinctive creative approach, the artist brings together ideas, emotions and visual storytelling to create meaningful experiences for viewers.
