Beyond the Canvas

2026 · Mixed Media

Beyond the Canvas explores the space between traditional art and experimental forms, bringing together painting, sculpture, photography and mixed media.

The exhibition challenges conventional ideas about where art begins and ends, creating an environment for experimentation and creative discovery.
